# Application Architecture

## Overview

Adritash provides application architecture consulting for enterprise systems — designing scalable, resilient applications aligned to business strategy and regulatory requirements.

## Business Problem

Enterprises often operate legacy application portfolios with unclear dependencies, inconsistent integration patterns, and architectures that cannot scale with business growth or cloud adoption.

## Solution

Adritash helps organisations assess application portfolios, define target architectures, establish governance, and plan modernization paths that reduce risk during transformation.

## Architecture

Typical engagement areas include:

- Enterprise architecture assessment
- Technology roadmap planning
- Application portfolio management
- Architecture governance and standards
- Integration pattern design

## Technology

Common technology domains covered:

- Enterprise platforms (PeopleSoft, Workday)
- APIs and integration (REST, GraphQL, event-driven patterns)
- Container platforms (Docker, ECS, Kubernetes patterns)
- Databases (PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Oracle, DynamoDB)

## Benefits

- Clearer architectural direction before major investment
- Reduced migration and modernization risk
- Standards that support cross-team delivery
