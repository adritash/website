# Consulting Overview

## Overview

Adritash provides technology architecture and consulting services through Dwaipayan Rajguru's practice, focused on enterprise outcomes in FinTech and regulated industries.

## Business Problem

Organisations need experienced architecture leadership for cloud modernization, AI adoption, and complex enterprise programmes — without adding permanent headcount before strategy is clear.

## Solution

Adritash offers consulting across three practice areas reflected on the public website:

1. **Enterprise Architecture** — assessment, roadmaps, governance, portfolio management
2. **Cloud** — multi-cloud migration and modernization (AWS, GCP, Azure)
3. **AI & Automation** — RAG, agentic workflows, LLM integration, intelligent automation

## Architecture

Engagements typically follow:

- Discovery and architecture assessment
- Target architecture and roadmap definition
- Delivery leadership across engineering teams (10–20 people)
- Vendor and stakeholder management

## Technology

Consulting spans the technology domains listed in Adritash skills content: enterprise platforms, cloud providers, AI tooling, databases, DevOps, and programme delivery.

## Benefits

Published portfolio themes include:

- Reduced infrastructure cost through cloud optimization
- High availability for mission-critical systems
- Faster knowledge retrieval and incident resolution through AI platforms

## Relevant Services

- Application Architecture
