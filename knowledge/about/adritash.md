# Adritash

## Overview

Adritash is a technology architecture and consulting platform founded by Dwaipayan Rajguru. The brand focuses on enterprise technology, application architecture, cloud modernization, AI, integration, and technical program management.

Adritash.com is the public website for the Adritash brand and Dwaipayan Rajguru's professional portfolio.
